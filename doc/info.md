International Medical Shadowing & Clinical Experience Program in Armenia
Discover Clinical Excellence in Armenia
We connect international medical students, pre-med students, residents, and young healthcare professionals with high-level clinical shadowing opportunities at Astghik Medical Center in Yerevan, Armenia.

Our program offers participants the opportunity to observe and learn from experienced surgeons and physicians across multiple specialties while gaining exposure to modern surgical techniques, patient care, and hospital systems in an international environment.

For advanced medical students and qualified participants, selected programs may also include supervised hands-on clinical experience during selected surgical procedures, strictly depending on academic level, qualifications, hospital approval, and local regulations.
About Astghik Medical Center
Astghik Medical Center is one of the largest, most modern, and most technologically advanced multidisciplinary hospitals in Armenia, located in the capital city of Yerevan.

Built according to European healthcare standards, the hospital is recognized for its modern infrastructure, advanced medical technologies, and internationally trained specialists.

The medical center includes more than 30 specialized departments and 15 state-of-the-art operating rooms equipped with advanced ventilation, sterilization, and surgical systems that meet international standards.

Astghik Medical Center actively collaborates with leading clinics and specialists from Europe, Russia, and the United States, allowing physicians to continuously exchange experience, participate in international trainings, and stay updated with the latest medical innovations and surgical techniques.

The center is internationally certified under ISO 9001:2015 quality management standards by TÜV Rheinland, reflecting its commitment to patient safety, healthcare quality, and clinical excellence.

Astghik Medical Center provides high-volume surgical and clinical practice in numerous specialties and serves thousands of patients every year.

Eligibility
Our programs are open to:
• Medical students from Years 1–6
• Pre-med students
• International healthcare trainees
• Young physicians and residents

Pre-med students are eligible for selected observational shadowing programs.

Advanced medical students may be eligible for supervised hands-on clinical exposure in selected surgeries and clinical activities depending on specialty, experience, and hospital approval.
Approximate Weekly Schedule
Monday to Friday:
• Observation of up to 5 surgeries daily
• Detailed explanations during surgeries
• Participation in post-operative discussions
• Exposure to post-operation patient care and guidance
• Clinical rounds and physician interaction
• Exposure to operating room protocols and surgical workflow

For advanced students:
• Supervised hands-on clinical exposure in selected surgeries and procedures
• Assistance during selected clinical activities where permitted
• Direct interaction with surgeons and medical teams
