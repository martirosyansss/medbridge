2. The programme

MedBridge organises international medical observerships at Astghik Medical Center in Yerevan, Armenia. The programme is intended for medical students, pre-medical students, healthcare trainees, residents, and young physicians.

3. Application is not a contract

Submitting the application form does not create a contract. Acceptance into the programme is at MedBridge's and Astghik Medical Center's sole discretion. A binding agreement is formed only when (a) MedBridge issues a written acceptance, (b) the participant signs the programme agreement, and (c) the programme fee has been received in cleared funds.

4. Observership scope and disclaimer

The programme is primarily observational. Participants are not licensed to practise medicine in Armenia and shall not provide patient treatment, diagnoses, or unsupervised clinical care. Supervised hands-on exposure is available only for qualified advanced students, subject to specialty, hospital approval, and Armenian law. Participants must follow hospital regulations, sterile-field protocols, and the instructions of supervising physicians at all times.

5. Fees, refunds, and rescheduling
